# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in this project.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Communicate constructively.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on improving the project collaboratively.

## Unacceptable Behavior
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disrespectful conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when these guidelines are violated.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and interactions related to the repository.
